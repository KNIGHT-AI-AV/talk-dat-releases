Talk Dat!

Privacy Notice

Effective August 2, 2026

Talk Dat! stays local-first. Local and bring-your-own-provider modes do not send desktop content to Knight AI+AV. Talk Dat! Cloud is optional and sends only released audio or a requested text operation through Knight's managed service and named processors.

What the account service stores

When account features are live and you choose to use them, the service stores your email address, Firebase user ID, trial dates, purchase and refund state, Stripe customer and transaction identifiers, a random Talk Dat! device identifier, a device name, activation timestamps, and entitlement version. It does not use a hardware fingerprint.

Payments

Stripe processes payment details. Knight receives transaction status and identifiers needed to issue, restore, support, or revoke an entitlement; Knight does not store complete card numbers.

Local data

Transcripts, protected voice sessions, history, dictionary entries, snippets, settings, and scratchpads remain under your Windows user profile. Provider keys and signed entitlement tokens are stored in Windows Credential Manager when available. Protected recovery recordings are not uploaded as an archive.

Managed cloud

If you explicitly select Talk Dat! Cloud, the released WAV is transiently sent through Knight's managed service to ElevenLabs for transcription. Requested managed formatting or translation text is transiently sent to Google Gemini. Knight meters aggregate audio seconds and text characters, but the managed route is designed not to store request audio or text in Firestore or application logs. Local and bring-your-own modes remain available when managed cloud is disabled, unavailable, or out of allowance.

Browser demo

The browser demo starts only after an explicit press or toggle. It does not save audio on the website. Depending on availability, speech may be handled by your browser's speech service or by Deepgram through a short-lived token. The shared Deepgram bridge stays disabled until abuse limits and billing controls are verified.

Retention and choices

Commerce records and aggregate managed-usage counters are retained as needed for allowances, licenses, fraud prevention, accounting, tax, refunds, and legal obligations. You can clear local content from the app or choose local/BYOK processing. For account access, correction, deletion, or export requests, contact Build@KnightAIAV.com. Some transaction records must be retained where law requires.

Security and changes

Account traffic uses HTTPS, server-side Firebase token verification, Stripe webhook signatures, and device-bound signed entitlements. No system is perfectly secure. Material changes to this notice will be dated here.
